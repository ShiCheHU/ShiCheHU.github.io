Selected Topics

主要研究方向

  • AI/ML for wireless air interface 面向无线空口的 AI/ML

    Research on AI-native CSI feedback and compression, beam management, channel representation, and intelligent physical-layer design for 5G-Advanced and beyond.

    聚焦 5G-Advanced 及后续演进中的 AI 原生 CSI 反馈与压缩、波束管理、信道表示以及智能物理层设计。

  • OFDM impairments and receiver algorithms OFDM 非理想影响与接收机算法

    Past academic work on phase noise mitigation, PAPR reduction, and low-complexity detection under non-ideal hardware constraints.

    围绕相位噪声补偿、峰均比抑制以及非理想硬件约束下的低复杂度检测算法开展研究。

  • Standardization and system studies 标准化与系统研究

    Work spanning 3GPP RAN1, Wi-Fi evolution, protocol study, and technical proposal development.

    持续参与 3GPP RAN1、Wi-Fi 演进、协议研究与技术提案撰写等工作。
