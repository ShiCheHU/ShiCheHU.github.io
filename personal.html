About Me

关于我

When I am not at work, I enjoy reading books, particularly biographies of historical figures, as well as works on history, economics, sociology, and psychology. I also love watching movies. Both books and films allow me to experience more than I could in my everyday life. I'm passionate about traveling and hiking — the beauty of natural landscapes and the grandeur of high mountains always refresh my mind and soothe my soul. I value friendship greatly. I enjoy spending time with friends, having meaningful conversations, and doing things together. Their wisdom continually inspires me to become a better person.

工作之余，我热爱阅读，尤其喜欢历史人物传记，以及历史、经济、社会学和心理学方面的著作。我也很喜欢看电影。书籍和电影都让我得以体验日常生活中无法触及的更多可能性。我热衷于旅行和徒步——大自然的美丽风光和高山的壮阔总能让我神清气爽、心灵宁静。我非常珍视友情，喜欢与朋友相聚、深入交流、共同做事。他们的智慧不断激励着我成为更好的人。

I deeply love my partner. We are highly compatible and grow even closer as time goes by.

我深爱我的伴侣。我们非常合拍，随着时间的推移，彼此的感情越发深厚。

I also dedicate time to practicing clerical script calligraphy and improving my English. I keep a daily journal and occasionally write blogs about technology.

我也坚持练习隶书书法和提升英语水平。我每天写日记，偶尔也会撰写技术博客。

此外，我保持规律的运动习惯，每周通过游泳和跑步等方式锻炼三小时。

Recent Books I've Read and Highly Recommend

近期读过并强烈推荐的书籍

Recently, I have been reading books about Zeng Guofan, a prominent statesman and military strategist of the late Qing Dynasty. He founded the Xiang Army, a regional militia, as a civil official and suppressed the Taiping Rebellion, rising to the highest ranks of the imperial court. In his later years, he dedicated himself to the Westernization Movement and facilitated the Chinese Educational Mission, which sent young students to study in the United States. His family letters, preserved to this day, along with his philosophical reflections on life and continuous self-improvement, remain worthy of study.

最近我在读关于曾国藩的书。他是晚清时期杰出的政治家和军事战略家，以文官之身创建了地方团练湘军，平定了太平天国运动，最终位极人臣。晚年的他致力于洋务运动，推动了中国留美幼童计划。他流传至今的家书以及关于人生的哲学思考和持续自我完善的精神，仍然值得今人学习。

Historical Figures and Related History历史人物及相关历史

  • Zeng Guofan's Family Letters 《曾国藩家书》 , by Zeng Guofan ，曾国藩 著
    • After Zeng Guofan began his post in the Hanlin Academy in Beijing, he started corresponding with his parents and siblings in his hometown. These letters capture his principles as a person, a scholar, and an official.
    曾国藩在北京翰林院任职后，开始与家乡的父母和兄弟姐妹通信，这些家书记录了他的为人、为学、为官之道。
  • Zeng Guofan 《曾国藩》 , by Tang Minghao ，唐浩明 著
    • In my opinion, this is the most comprehensive and impartial biography of Zeng Guofan I've come across. The author has dedicated himself to studying Zeng since his PhD, making him a true authority on the subject.
    在我看来，这是我读过的最全面、最客观的曾国藩传记。作者自博士阶段起便专注于曾国藩研究，是该领域真正的权威。
  • The Taiping Revolution Movement 《太平天国革命运动》 , by Jen Yu-wen ，简又文 著
    • This is one of the few books that details the entire history of the Taiping Rebellion, from start to finish. Reading it alongside a biography of Zeng Guofan pairs perfectly for grasping the bigger picture of late Qing society.
    这是为数不多完整详述太平天国运动始末的著作。与曾国藩传记一起阅读，可以更好地理解晚清社会的全貌。
