About

简介

I work on wireless communication standardization, with a focus on AI/ML for the air interface, CSI feedback and compression, beam management, and integrated sensing and communication. My work spans 3GPP RAN1 standardization, wireless system design, and algorithm development for OFDM and MIMO systems.

我目前从事无线通信标准化工作，重点方向包括空口 AI/ML、CSI 反馈与压缩、波束管理以及通感一体化。我长期参与 3GPP RAN1 标准化工作，同时关注 OFDM 与 MIMO 系统中的无线算法与系统设计。

I received my Ph.D. from the Shanghai Advanced Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My doctoral research focused on non-ideal hardware impairments in communication systems, especially phase noise mitigation and PAPR reduction in OFDM. I have industry experience from both a major mobile operator and a leading device manufacturer.

我毕业于中国科学院上海高等研究院，博士期间主要研究通信系统中的非理想硬件影响，尤其是 OFDM 系统中的相位噪声抑制和峰均比降低问题。我也曾在移动运营商和终端厂商从事研发与标准相关工作。

Selected Research

研究摘要

  • AI/ML for air interface and CSI compression 空口 AI/ML 与 CSI 压缩
    3GPP RAN1, 5G-Advanced to 6G
    3GPP RAN1，面向 5G-Advanced 与 6G

    Research and standardization contributions on AI-native CSI feedback/compression, beam management, and ML-assisted physical-layer design.

    围绕 AI 原生 CSI 反馈/压缩、波束管理以及物理层智能化设计开展研究与标准化贡献。

  • OFDM impairments and optimization OFDM 非理想影响与优化
    Phase noise mitigation, PAPR reduction, blind detection
    相位噪声抑制、PAPR 降低、盲检测

    我也会持续整理无线标准和协议演进相关的技术笔记与学习材料。
